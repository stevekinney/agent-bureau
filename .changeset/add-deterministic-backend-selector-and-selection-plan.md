Adds the deterministic backend selector and the self-contained `SelectionPlan` (AB-64, implemented by AB-249).

`packages/operative/src/providers/selection.ts` is new and exports `TaskClassification`, `SelectionRequest`, `SelectionCandidate`, `SelectionOutcomeKind`, `SelectionOutcomeFailure`, `SelectionPlan`, `EffectiveGenerationResult`, `SelectOptions`, `RevalidationInput`, `select(request, options)`, and `recordEffectiveGeneration(plan, effective)`. `select` is a pure, synchronous function of `request`'s recorded signature (`catalogRevision`, `policyRevision`, `availabilitySnapshotRevision`, `taskClassification`, `requestedValue`) and `options`'s catalog and five policy layers: it calls `composePolicy` (AB-248) to filter hard constraints, applies effort compatibility within the eligible set, then ranks by `costPreference`/`latencyPreference`/`preferredProviders`/`preferredModels`, breaking ties by `(provider, model)` lexicographic order. Two calls with identical recorded inputs produce deeply equal plans, including `planId` when an injected `newPlanId` is supplied.

Every eligible candidate's `descriptorSnapshot` is a deep, independent structural copy of the deciding `BackendDescriptor` — never a live reference into the catalog — so a `SelectionPlan` replays its own eligibility reasoning unchanged after the source catalog has moved on, been mutated, or been discarded. `rankingInputs` is populated for every eligible candidate with three named signals (`cost`, `latency`, `preferenceMatch`), never summarized into one opaque score.

All seven `SelectionOutcomeKind` values (`selected`, `no-candidate`, `stale-catalog`, `capability-changed`, `policy-changed`, `exact-override-rejected`, `provider-effective-divergence`) are reachable and covered; `failure` is present if and only if `outcome !== 'selected'`, enforced structurally by a two-arm discriminated union. An exact override rejected by any layer above the user's yields a single-candidate plan with `eligible: false`, that layer's `exclusionCode`, and `outcome: 'exact-override-rejected'` carrying `rejectedOverride`. An unsupported effort defaults to a typed `'incompatible-effort'` rejection; `effortFallbackMode: 'degrade'` selects the nearest supported lower tier from the candidate's own `descriptor.effort.degradesTo` table and records the degraded tier on `selected.effort`, never silently. `recordEffectiveGeneration` folds a completed generation's actual effective backend into a plan without ever rewriting `selected`: a divergence is recorded as a new terminal `'provider-effective-divergence'` plan alongside the original selection.

`TaskClassification` stays the opaque caller-supplied tag AB-64 fixes — no taxonomy, no quality-evidence-based classification (out of scope per ABP-11's ruling); `SelectionCandidate.rankingInputs` is the named extension point for a future signal. `SelectionRequest.requestedValue` reuses AB-67's shipped `SteeringRequestedValue`, imported type-only, narrowed to `'model' | 'provider' | 'route' | 'effort'`.

No `any` and no `as unknown as`.
